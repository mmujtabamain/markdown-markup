One page, two kinds of target

The same Extended Mark page, drawn by two kinds of target: on the plain web, and in React. Run time draws an IR built ahead, with no compiler in the page; real time compiles the text in the browser, with xtmd.wasm, every time it changes. Vue, Svelte, Solid and Angular have the same pair.
