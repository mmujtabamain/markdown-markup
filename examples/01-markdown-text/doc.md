# A trip to Lisbon

Lisbon sits on **seven hills** above the Tagus, and its trams are *still* the easiest way up.[^trams]
The [city's tourism office](https://www.visitlisboa.com) lists the main sights.

## Before you go

1. Check the tram timetable.
2. Pack shoes for cobbles.
3. Learn three words: *obrigado*, *bom dia*, *café*.

## On the day

- [x] Ride tram 28 to the castle
- [ ] Watch the sunset from a *miradouro*[^view]
- [ ] Eat a *pastel de nata* in Belém

### Getting around

Most neighbourhoods are walkable; the hills are the hard part.

[^trams]: Tram 28 has run the same route since 1914.

[^view]: A *miradouro* is a viewpoint; the city has more than twenty.
