## New
- **`segments(source)`** (in `mkp.js`, `mkp-outline.js` and `mkp-dom.js`): a document's pieces in order, each its source lines as written: prose, each top-level widget with its place and name, and the front matter, definitions and state blocks a widget may need to compile on its own.
- **`\val{}` written in a document's prose** is warned of (`mkp.val-in-prose`): it is shown as written; live values work in a widget's text.
- **A bundle carries its images** (`assets`, digests in its lock): a pack's images its widgets name are drawn from the bundle, never fetched, so its design is frozen with it; React Native draws a carried SVG.

## Fixed
- **`minheight` with `aspect`** made a box as wide as that shape needs (720 px in a 372 px column) and let taller content spill over the next block. A spacer inside the widget now reserves the room, so the box is the larger of that and its content, never wider than its column; the placeholder is never wider than its column either.
- **A widget's own lone size default** is warned of once, where it is declared, not on every use.
- **A host's extern** is spaced in a document as a widget is (`data-mkp-ext`).
- **`places: true`:** a `state` block has a place, so its controls are drawn in its box; footnotes are the page's prose; a place with no box is said of once.
## Changed
- **Breaking:** `meta.place` counts `state` blocks too, so widgets after one are numbered one higher (old bundles are renumbered by `mkp migrate`).
