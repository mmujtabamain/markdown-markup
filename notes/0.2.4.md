## New
- **Arrows cross less and hop where they must:** tracks in a gap are ordered to cross least, lines never hug each other, and a remaining crossing gets a small hop. `arrow hop=none` for lifelines and rules.
- **Icons `more` and `less`**, the chevrons down and up beside `prev` and `next`: a disclosure writes `icon name=(if(open, more, next))`.
- **`arrow lanes=turn`**: a via lane says where a routed line turns, without pulling it to the lane's middle.
- **`styles: 'external'`** on `<MkpDocument>`, `mount()`, `firstPaint()` and `--target fragment --styles external`, with `mkp.css` in the web zip: many documents on one page share one stylesheet.
- **`mkp-page.js`** (`firstPaint()` for a Node build) in the web zip, and the React Native renderer as `mkp-react-native-<version>.zip`.
## Changed
- Routed arrows' turns in a shared gap keep 8 px apart where they can, and a gap too narrow for them marks its arrows `data-crowded`.
- Printed YAML quotes `yes`, `no`, `on`, `off`, `y` and `n`.
## Fixed
- `mkp migrate`: a pack given both to migrate and with `--pack` is taken once; documents aren't written when their pack fails; `--check` says what it would change, or "up to date"; a document migrated after its pack is told why it fails; defaults are no longer written into YAML; the migrate proof skips fixture cases meant to fail.
